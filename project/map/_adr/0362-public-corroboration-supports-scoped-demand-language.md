# ADR 0362: Public corroboration supports scoped demand language

**Status**: accepted

## Context

[ADR 0292](0292-the-demand-canon-counters-the-benefit-canon.md) seeded the demand canon before launch and deliberately classified every struggling moment as a hypothesis. It allowed three market evidence classes: observational for internal evidence, anecdotal for a named external person's permitted account, and hypothesis for an untested interpretation.

The first evidence sweep exposed a missing state. Several demand patterns were present in artifact-backed founder experience and in multiple concrete public accounts across GitHub, Hacker News, Reddit, and a vendor engineering report. That corpus was stronger than an untested hypothesis, but it was neither internal observation nor a named testimonial for which discern held permission. Leaving it as hypothesis would tell a future writer that no external test had occurred. Calling it anecdotal or observational would misstate the source.

Public discussion is also not a representative sample. Repetition across a few forums cannot establish how common a problem is, which segment feels it most, whether it causes an outcome, or whether anyone will buy a solution. The evidence system needs to recognize qualitative corroboration without laundering it into research-grade validation.

## Decision

**The claims ledger gains a `corroborated` evidence class for tightly scoped qualitative market patterns found independently in a recorded public corpus.**

- Corroborated demand evidence carries at least three distinct public sources across at least two venues. Each source records its venue, descriptive title, and canonical HTTPS location. The evidence row also records the corpus's limits as a complete sentence.
- Corroboration permits non-quantified, segment-scoped recognition language. It cannot support prevalence, causation, typical outcomes, endorsement, product effectiveness, or a quotation presented as a testimonial.
- Founder testimony and repository artifacts may strengthen the account and verify its chronology or mechanism. They do not substitute for the external corpus, and remembered details remain labelled as recollection when no surviving artifact measures them.
- Contrary cases and conditions belong in the private evidence sweep before a demand entry is promoted. The typed registry guards the mechanical corpus minimum; editorial judgment still decides whether the sources describe the same bounded pattern and are independent enough to count.
- Named or quoted external experience remains anecdotal and permission-bound. Formal interviews, surveys, and behavioral market evidence can later promote or narrow the demand account further.

The initial sweep promotes only the entries reached by its three corpora: coordination and review becoming limiting work; project context failing to travel automatically across sessions or providers; and completion statements lacking durable verification evidence. The private claims residue holds the full founder accounts, artifact audit, source corpus, contrary cases, strongest public form, and forbidden inferences.

## Consequences

- A writing agent no longer has to choose between calling these three scoped patterns untested and overstating them as established market prevalence.
- The generated demand canon makes the source corpus and limits visible beside every corroborated entry. Reusing one corpus across related entries remains explicit rather than inflating the number of independent observations.
- Public copy can recognize a supported situation directly, but numbers, universals, customer outcomes, and product-efficacy claims still require their own evidence.
- The corpus is directional pre-launch evidence. Interviews remain necessary to learn incidence, intensity, language, alternatives, buying triggers, and willingness to pay.
- Adding a future market evidence class enrolls deliberately through the shared ledger and the demand-canon parity guard.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the entries as hypotheses until formal interviews.** Rejected because it falsely records that no external test occurred and recreates the drafting deadlock the evidence system should resolve.
- **Classify the corpus as anecdotal.** Rejected because that class governs a named person's attributable result and permission, not a non-testimonial synthesis of several public accounts.
- **Classify the founder account as observational and ignore the public corpus.** Rejected because it would permit only attributed internal use and would discard the evidence that the pattern appears outside discern.
- **Call the class validated.** Rejected because qualitative public discussion does not validate prevalence, segmentation, or demand for the product.
