# Understand

These pages build the mental models behind the practice: what discern's states mean, who holds which authority, and why the design draws its boundaries where it does. Come here when the question is conceptual — what did green establish, who may land this change, why did the evidence expire. When you want to do something, use the [Guides](../10-guides/README.md); for an exact field, flag, or term, use [Reference](../30-reference/README.md); when something has gone wrong, start from [Troubleshooting](../40-troubleshooting/README.md).

If you read one page, read [Proof](proof.md). It answers the questions that decide whether delegated work can be trusted: what a green Gate establishes, how green differs from landed, who authorizes a landing, and why evidence goes stale.

## In this section

- [Practice and roles](practice-and-roles.md): discern as a project-installed practice, the coding agent as its operator, and you as owner and reviewer. Start here if the pieces haven't clicked together yet.
- [Proof](proof.md): what green establishes, the road from green to released, who authorizes a landing, and why Proof goes stale.
- [Checkpoints](checkpoints.md): judgment questions served at the change, declared answers kept apart from machine results, and the variance only you can authorize.
- [Standards](standards.md): quality measures held at limits that can only improve, and why a passing number records earned ground rather than a score.
- [Worktrees and trunk](worktrees-and-trunk.md): why unfinished work stays in its own workspace, and how update, composition, and acceptance move work and evidence.
- [Instructions, Skills, and the Map](instructions-skills-and-map.md): the durable homes for project knowledge, and which writing belongs to the project, the agents, or this manual.
- [Local control](local-control.md): the network, model, and write boundaries, and what discern does not secure or decide.
- [Evidence and improvement](evidence-and-improvement.md): what the local record can support, and where comparison stops short of causation or ranking.
