# Understand

As you hand more work to agents, it helps to know what their checks show, what your project remembers, and which decisions stay with you. These pages explain each idea through situations you'll recognize, so the next result your agent brings back is easier to judge.

- [How discern works](how-discern-works.md) shows how you, your agent, and your project share the work.

To answer a particular question, pick a page below.

## What has your agent finished?

- [Proof](proof.md): which checks passed, on which version of the code, and what's left for you to judge.
- [Worktrees and trunk](worktrees-and-trunk.md): where unfinished work lives, and how a reviewed change joins your shared branch.
- [Checkpoints](checkpoints.md): how a review question reaches your agent when a certain change happens, and when its answer needs you.

## What can your project keep for next time?

- [Standards](standards.md): how a measured improvement becomes a limit that later changes must meet.
- [Instructions, skills, and the map](instructions-skills-and-map.md): how rules, methods, and project knowledge outlast the session that produced them.
- [Learn from your project's history](evidence-and-improvement.md): how local records help you and your agent find what keeps slowing work down.

## What stays under your control?

- [What stays on your machine](local-control.md): what discern runs, writes, and records on your machine, and how that differs from what your coding agent does.

When you're ready to act, the [guides](../10-guides/README.md) walk you through each task. [Reference](../30-reference/README.md) has the exact fields, commands, and settings.
