# Understand

These pages explain the ideas behind discern, so the next time your agent says a change is done, you'll know what to look at before you decide whether it lands.

For the overview of who does what, start with [How discern works](../00-start/how-discern-works.md).

## What has your agent finished?

- [Proof](proof.md): which of your project's commands passed on which exact commit, and what's left for you to judge.
- [Worktrees and trunk](worktrees-and-trunk.md): where unfinished work lives, and how a reviewed change joins your shared branch.
- [Checkpoints](checkpoints.md): review questions your agent answers when certain files change, and which answers need your decision.

## What can your project keep for next time?

- [Standards](standards.md): how a measured gain becomes a limit every later change must meet.
- [Instructions, skills, and the map](instructions-skills-and-map.md): how what you teach one session reaches every later one.
- [Improve how your agents work](../20-guides/improve-the-practice.md): finding what keeps slowing work down, from discern's local record.

## What stays under your control?

- [What stays on your machine](local-control.md): what discern runs, records, and writes locally, and where your agent and your commands go beyond it.

The [guides](../20-guides/README.md) walk you through each task, and [Reference](../30-reference/README.md) has exact commands and settings.
