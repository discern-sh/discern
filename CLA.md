# discern contributor license agreements

## Why this agreement exists

discern is Fair Source software. It is distributed under the [Functional Source License](LICENSE), each release converts to Apache-2.0 two years after that release, and internal use is free for everyone, businesses included. Keeping that model working means one party must be able to license the complete work: to publish each release's Apache-2.0 conversion, to sell commercial licenses, and to adjust terms if the model ever needs to change.

This agreement is what makes that possible. You keep the copyright in everything you contribute. You grant the project's steward a broad license to use your contribution as part of discern and to license it onward under any terms, commercial terms included. The steward is Jack Webb-Heller, and the grant extends to the steward's successors and assigns, so the project can later pass to a company without asking every contributor again. The grant is non-exclusive: your work stays yours, and nothing here limits what you do with your own code outside discern.

You sign once. On your first pull request, the CLA assistant asks for a signature, and one reply covers that contribution and every later one.

The individual agreement covers work you own. If you contribute work your employer owns, your employer signs the corporate agreement as well.

Both agreements are adapted from The Apache Software Foundation's Individual and Corporate Contributor License Agreements (ICLA v2.2 and CCLA r190612).
