# Code of Conduct

discern's project spaces are for useful, good-faith discussion and collaboration. What matters here is the work and how people treat each other while doing it.

Be friendly, constructive, and respectful. Disagreement is welcome but personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of someone else's private information, and deliberate disruption are not.

This applies to discern's issue tracker and other project-managed spaces. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or block participation when necessary. Serious conduct may result in an immediate ban.

To report a concern privately, email [hello@discern.sh](mailto:hello@discern.sh).
