"**Snowline**" is discern's v1.2.0 release, focused on a redesigned desk: the interactive view of every task in progress, which opens when you run `discern` in your main checkout.

## A redesigned desk

- The desk opens on Commands, so starting a task, reading the manual, running a script, and checking for updates are in view from the first screen.
- Tasks are grouped by who moves next: Ready for review, Needs attention, Working, Approved to land, and Idle. A details panel follows your selection, and Enter runs the task's next step.
- The desk, `discern status`, and `discern enter` describe each task in the same words, and a green mark only ever means work that can land.
- Reviews lead with what a change will do. The changes, the plan, and the exact command are one key away.
- Checks and landings run inside the desk with live progress, and Session activity lists what the desk ran and how it ended.
- The manual opens inside the desk, and `discern docs` uses the same reader.
- An idle desk does far less work: it surveys your tasks only when something has changed.

## Other fixes and changes

- `discern done` no longer crashes when its terminal stops reading output for a while. If another run takes over meanwhile, `done` ends as cancelled and says why.
- A check that prints a very long line no longer slows the live view of `discern done` or the desk.
- `discern doctor` and other commands wrap long advice to the width of your terminal.
- A task with no commits reads Empty when the trunk moves, and a branch whose commits continue in another task stays Contained however long it idles.
- A landing that stops after the trunk has moved says what landed, and the desk offers recovery steps for an interrupted landing.
- The CI guide asks you to leave GitHub's **Require linear history** rule off for your trunk, because a task that lands after the trunk has moved arrives as a merge commit.
- `discern status --json` rows gain `state` and `group` fields, and `discern progress` reports each step of a plan as it runs.
- The manual is shorter and plainer, with overlapping pages merged.
