"**Snowline**" is discern's v1.2.0 release, focused on a redesigned desk: the interactive view of every task in progress, which opens when you run `discern` in your main checkout.

## A redesigned desk

The desk has been rebuilt from the ground up. The new desk significantly improves usability and developer experience. Highlights include:

- Easier access to everyday commands: start a task, read the manual, run a script, check for updates, and more.
- Clearer statuses: tasks are grouped by their state, so you can clearly see what's currently in progress, ready for review, or needs your attention.  
- Live progress: checks and landings run inside the desk with realtime progress updates.
- Help and assistance: an improved documentation reader is now integrated in the desk itself, as well as `discern docs`.

## Other fixes and changes

- The manual is shorter and plainer, with overlapping pages merged.
- Long-running `discern done` commands are more reliable.
- `discern doctor` and other commands wrap long advice to the width of your terminal.
- The CI guide asks you to leave GitHub's **Require linear history** rule off for your trunk, because a task that lands after the trunk has moved arrives as a merge commit.
- `discern status --json` rows gain `state` and `group` fields, and `discern progress` reports each step of a plan as it runs.
