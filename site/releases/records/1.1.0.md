Welcome to "**Clearwater**": discern's v1.1.0 release.

## A manual in your own words

- The manual now uses the words you already use for your project, such as tests, linter, and commit, and carries each example through to its outcome, with real output where it helps.
- Understand now comes before Guides, so you meet the ideas before the tasks that use them. Web addresses haven't changed.
- `discern --help` gives one short line per command, and glossary cards appear on discern's own terms.

## Emergency landings show everything you approve

- The plan lists every commit the repair would land, including another task's unlanded work that comes with it, and your approval covers exactly that list.
- A looser standard limit or an unmet checkpoint can take the emergency route only with its own approval, as in an ordinary landing. An emergency landing still never counts as Proof.

## Fixes

- A landing whose Proof note couldn't be written keeps its worktree. Run `discern accept` there again to record the note; the change doesn't land twice.
- `discern status`, the desk, and `discern enter` mark a worktree whose env file they can't read, instead of crashing.
- `discern upgrade` reports the schema version it started from, and `discern skills list --markdown` keeps excluded and overriding skills.

## Changed behavior

- `discern uninstall --json` and `--markdown` refuse with `confirmation_required` until you pass `--yes`, so a script that applies the removal must add it.
- Recovering an interrupted landing after the trunk moved now finishes and cleans up, and `discern worktree prune` keeps a worktree whose landing is still settling.
