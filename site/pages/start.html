The pattern

You've probably lived this one.

Those teams stopped trusting their own confidence long ago: they run checks on every change, automatically, because software breaks in the exact spot nobody looked at. How it works

Your agent gets a studio. Your app gets checks.

discern helps your agent check its own homework. It lives inside your project, your agent operates it, every change happens in a draft, every "done" gets checked first — and you approve what gets kept.

drafts

Changes happen in a copy

Your agent works on a draft of the app, never the one that's live. If an idea goes sideways, your working version was never in danger.

engineers call these worktrees

checks

"Done" has to pass first

Before your agent calls anything finished, your project's checks run on the final version. A failure tells the agent exactly what broke, so the next fix is aimed, and checked again.

no more "everything's working"

memory

Your project remembers

The rules of your project are written down where every session reads them. A new chat doesn't mean starting the explanation over.

works across different agents too

Is it safe?

Fair questions, straight answers.

Is this another AI watching my AI?

No. There's no model inside discern and nothing that guesses. It runs your project's checks and reports what they said. It can't hallucinate a result.

Does my project get uploaded somewhere?

No. discern runs on your computer and makes zero network calls. No telemetry, no account, and it keeps working with the wifi off.

Do I have to learn developer stuff?

No. Your agent operates discern for you. If you're curious, ask it what a check just did. It's a patient explainer, and the vocabulary comes free.

What if I want it gone?

Tell your agent to uninstall it. The wiring comes out and your project stays, along with the notes and rules it wrote, which are worth keeping either way.

For the agent reading along

Every discern verb takes --json and returns one typed result. Setup asks the human's consent before writing anything. The manual written for you is DISCERN(1) — plaintext at /llms.txt.
